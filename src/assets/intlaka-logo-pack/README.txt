INTLAKA LOGO PACK

mark/      The logo mark alone, transparent background.
           black = use on light backgrounds, white = use on dark backgrounds.
           SVG for the website and print, PNG for documents and slides.

favicon/   favicon.svg and favicon.ico: white mark on a black circle,
           visible on both light and dark browser tabs.
           favicon-adaptive.svg: optional, no background, switches black/white
           with the system theme (not supported in every browser).
           apple-touch-icon.png, android-chrome-*.png, maskable-icon-512.png,
           site.webmanifest and head-snippet.html for the website.
           apple-touch-icon and maskable-icon are full squares on purpose:
           iOS and Android apply their own mask shape to them.

social/    Profile pictures (LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp), 800x800,
           dark and light versions. Platforms crop them to a circle; the mark
           stays inside the safe area.

Colors: #111111 (ink), #ffffff (white), #f4f4f2 (light background).
